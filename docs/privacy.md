# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy covers this website, www.athelstanservices.co.uk. Our apps have their own policies, linked from their pages.

## The short version

We collect nothing, sell nothing and share nothing. This site has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no forms and no accounts.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub sees your IP address when your browser fetches a page, and may keep it in its logs for security. We don't receive or use those logs. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The page also loads a small script from Cloudflare's cdnjs service, which links the page headings. Cloudflare sees your IP address when it serves the script. It sets no cookies.

## If you email us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We never add you to a mailing list, and we never sell or share your details. We delete emails when they are no longer needed.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email [info@athelstanservices.co.uk](mailto:info@athelstanservices.co.uk). You can also complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
