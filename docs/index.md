# Athelstan Services Ltd

**Email security and messaging expertise, and simple, private apps.**

Athelstan Services is a UK company with long experience in email security, including work behind more than 25 patents in the field, covering areas such as domain impersonation detection, malware detection and login credential warnings.

## What we do

- **Consultancy:** cloud services, secure email gateways, email archiving, messaging and groupware.
- **Apps:** small, privacy-first apps that collect no data.

## Our apps

- **[SafeSite](https://tylersimon.github.io/SafeSitePublic/)**: warns you before you type a password or card details on a site you haven't marked as safe. For Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and for Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
- **[Tick Lists](https://tylersimon.github.io/TickListsPublic/)**: a simple checklist for shopping, packing and to-dos, on iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch.
